# Public Transport System
## Overview
In this modern era, public transportation is a very important part of daily life. Our project is about creating a
centralized software application for the Public Transport System in Punjab, which includes the Metro buses, 
Speedo buses, and the new Electro buses. Right now, managing these buses is done very manually. We want to make
a system that helps passengers travel easily and helps the management control the bus schedules without any confusion.
## Scope
High Level Features:
Live GPS Tracking of all buses.
Online Ticketing System using QR codes.
Database Management System for Punjab Mass Transit Authority, maintaining reports of 
ridership (only those using the app) per day.
## Stakeholders
1- Passengers: They want to reach their destination on time, pay easily, and have a comfortable journey.
2- Punjab Mass transit Authority (PMTA): They want to control all the buses, check how much money is being made (via app). 
   They can track if the drivers follow the route and schedule.
3- System Database: It needs to store all the passenger accounts and ticket records securely so the system doesn't crash.
## Functional requirements
- FR-01: Live Bus Tracking: The system shall allow passengers to track the live GPS location of Metro, 
         Speedo, and Electro buses on their routes.
- FR-02: Online Ticketing: The system shall allow passengers to purchase bus tickets online using QR codes
         and scan them at buses or stations.
- FR-03: Ridership Reports: The system shall allow the Punjab Mass Transit Authority (PMA) to view daily 
         passenger ridership reports of users who purchase tickets through the application.
## Nonfunctional requirements
- NFR-01: Performance: The application shall open the bus tracker within 10 seconds, even during rush hours.
- NFR-02: Usability: The application shall have a user-friendly and easy-to-understand interface so passengers
          can use its features easily.
- NFR-03: Reliability: The system shall securely store passenger accounts and ticket records and maintain
          reliable operation during normal usage.
## Success criteria
- The application should be very user-friendly and fast.
- Passengers satisfaction should be atleast 60 percent.
- It should reduce the long ques by 30%.
- The Passenger can open app’s tracker in 10 seconds during rush hours.
- At least 7000 people should download the app in the first month.
